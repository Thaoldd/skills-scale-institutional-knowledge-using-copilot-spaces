# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This guide brings together OctoAcme's project management processes and explains how they fit across a project lifecycle. The guidance applies to cross-functional projects delivering product features, services, or integrations. Teams should use it as a shared reference and adapt details such as meeting cadence to their agreed working practices.

## Process at a glance

1. **Initiate** — Validate the business need, define a measurable goal and success criteria, identify stakeholders and risks, and decide whether the work is ready for planning.
2. **Plan** — Turn approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, responsibilities, dependencies, a Definition of Done, and a release and milestone plan.
3. **Execute and track** — Deliver in small increments, track work on the project board, test and review changes, monitor progress and risks, and communicate blockers and status.
4. **Release and deploy** — Confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback plans are ready; deploy, verify, and communicate the release.
5. **Reflect and improve** — Hold retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, and incidents. Record a small set of owned action items and follow up on their impact.

Risk management and stakeholder communication support every phase. Clear roles and ownership help teams make decisions and keep work moving throughout the lifecycle.
